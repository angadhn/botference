What you are looking at

These are your projects, shown two ways; switch between them under View. The map shows each project as a dot in one of four rough groups (writing, teaching, research and ventures, other), guessed from its title; being near each other means nothing more. The city shows the same projects as buildings you can drag into your own arrangement: lit windows are readings updated in the last month, shutters mean paused or archived. A line on the map, or a footpath in the city, means a connection between two projects was recorded: solid when you linked them or your writing uses one in the other, dashed when an agent only suggested it. A faint grey line on the map only means both projects hold the same reading or piece of writing. Click a project to see its readings, writing and chats; the Back button returns you to where you were.
