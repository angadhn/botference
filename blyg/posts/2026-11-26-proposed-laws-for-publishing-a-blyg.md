# Proposed laws for publishing a blyg

When botference publishes a blyg post for its owner, it makes promises. The [laws page](https://botference.com/laws.html) writes each promise as one sentence. For each law, it says where the promise comes from and how it is checked today.

These laws are proposals. The owner has not adopted any of them. Only the owner can adopt, edit or reject a law; an AI may only propose one.

Each law starts with "For every". For example, one law says that a run of the draft-filling script leaves the draft file exactly as the owner left it. Fifteen laws were proposed on 26 November 2026.

Some laws are not checked by a test yet, and the page says so for each one. An adopted law will get an independent check, written from the law and not from the code. A copy of the laws for review is in the repository, at [docs/laws.md](https://github.com/angadhn/botference/blob/main/docs/laws.md).
